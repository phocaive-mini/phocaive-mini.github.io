2. Retention and deletion

  • Account information, records and photos are kept until you delete your account and are deleted right away when you do. They may remain in disaster-recovery backups for up to 7 days before being deleted automatically.
  • Screen usage and error information are deleted after the period set by Google (up to 90 days).
  • Deleted information cannot be recovered.

4. Your rights

  • You can edit or delete your records in the app, and change your nickname in [More → My profile].
  • You can delete all your information at any time in [More → My profile → Delete account].
  • On Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID in [Settings → Google → Ads]. On iOS, we do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). In regions where ad consent is required, you can change your choice in the privacy options under [More], and we do not collect screen usage in those regions.
  • For any other request to access, correct or delete your information, email us below and we will respond within 10 days.

5. Security

All communication is encrypted. Passwords are stored encrypted by the authentication service, and even the Company cannot see them. Server access is limited to the operator.

6. Children under 14

Children under 14 cannot sign up as members.
